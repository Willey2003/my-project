# Phase 21 (optional) - RHCA stretch

Schedule only after the core path. By then you hold EX280, EX380 and EX430, which count toward the OpenShift-track RHCA. Before choosing electives, check the current RHCA track requirements on redhat.com (they changed to track-specific rules). Budget 6-8 weeks per elective at the same 3-5 h/day rhythm, one elective at a time, and reuse this kit's CRC environment.

Decision rule from your Career sheet: pursue RHCA if you target Red Hat-heavy GCC/BFSI/telecom Principal roles; skip it for product-company Staff/Principal roles, where the capstone and system-design practice pay off more.
