# DevOps study prep (Senior/Principal path)

Built from the Career Master Plan workbook: 70 core weeks, 20 phases, 786 hours, studied at 3-5 hours a day.

| Folder | What |
|---|---|
| `schedule/` | `Daily_Study_Schedule_3-5h.xlsx`: one row per day from Mon 28 Sep 2026 to Mon 12 Apr 2027 (Mon-Fri 4 h, Sat 5 h, Sun 3 h), plus exam target dates |
| `study-guides/` | one guide per phase: concepts, commands, labs, self-check, resources (CKAD guide still to come; CKAD days use the CKA guide) |
| `lab/` | the lab environment, one folder per component and one Kubernetes namespace per component. Start with `lab/README.md` and `./lab/lab.sh doctor` |

The analyzer app at the repo root (`app.py`, `tests/`, `devops-assistant.yaml`) is unchanged.
